The catalogue in `src/data/exercises.json` is authoritative for editorial names, movement descriptions, equipment, and search tags. The CDC source titled “Toe Lift” demonstrates rising onto the balls of the feet; its catalogue label is “Standing calf raise”. The half squat is performed without a chair or hand support. Descriptions identify the demonstrated movement and do not prescribe dosage or establish clinical suitability. The download script restores missing video files without rewriting catalogue metadata or these credits.
